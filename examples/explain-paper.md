# Example: Explain the Paper

This paper is relevant to energy system planning because it targets integrated community energy systems under renewable uncertainty, load variability, and equipment failure risk. Instead of planning only for economic cost and checking reliability afterward, it embeds reliability cost directly into the planning objective, forming a joint planning model of economy and reliability.

The method coordinates electricity, heating, cooling, and gas resources, including PV, wind turbines, CHP, heat pumps, boilers, chillers, storage, and integrated flexible loads. Its computational contribution is the L-shaped with state similarity method: scenarios with the same optimal-basis structure are grouped, one representative scenario is optimized, and the rest are derived through linear equations.

Open-source code: https://github.com/JAVIQU666/JAVIQU666-energy_system_planning
