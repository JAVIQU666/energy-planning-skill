# Example: Literature Review Entry

Qu, Jiawei, Zeyu Liu, Kai Hou, Yue Zhou, Lewei Zhu, Xiaohong Dong, Yunfei Mu, and Hongjie Jia. "Joint planning of economy and reliability for integrated community energy systems: A similarity-based massive scenario optimization approach." *Applied Energy* 381 (2025): 125054.

This paper proposes a joint planning model of economy and reliability for integrated community energy systems. The model incorporates renewable energy uncertainty, multi-energy load variation, and equipment failure through normal and N-k contingency scenarios. To address the computational burden of massive scenario optimization, the authors combine L-shaped decomposition with a state similarity method that solves representative scenarios and derives related scenario solutions through linear equations.
