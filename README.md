# Joint planning of economy and reliability for integrated community energy systems

This repository contains an agent-readable publication package for the paper:

**Joint planning of economy and reliability for integrated community energy systems: A similarity-based massive scenario optimization approach**

Authors: Jiawei Qu, Zeyu Liu, Kai Hou, Yue Zhou, Lewei Zhu, Xiaohong Dong, Yunfei Mu, Hongjie Jia

Published in: *Applied Energy*, Volume 381, Article 125054, 2025

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2024.125054

Open-source code: https://github.com/JAVIQU666/JAVIQU666-energy_system_planning

## One-line summary

This paper proposes a joint economy and reliability planning model for integrated community energy systems and accelerates massive scenario optimization with a state similarity method.

## What this paper contributes

- A joint planning model of economy and reliability for integrated community energy systems.
- A bi-level planning framework using the L-shaped method.
- A state similarity method that reduces the computational cost of massive operational scenarios.
- An operational framework considering N-k contingencies, renewable uncertainty, multi-energy load variation, and integrated flexible loads.
- Evidence that the L-shaped with state similarity method can improve computational efficiency by more than tenfold.

## Suitable for questions about

- Integrated community energy system planning
- Economy and reliability co-optimization
- Massive scenario optimization
- N-k contingency planning
- Multi-energy systems
- Renewable energy uncertainty
- Flexible load modeling
- L-shaped decomposition
- State similarity methods
- Energy storage planning and reliability

## Key result

The planning framework with N-k scenarios and integrated flexible loads reduced investment cost by 16.53%, operational cost by 10.7%, and total cost by 14.71% in the reported case study.
