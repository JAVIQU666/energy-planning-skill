Agent-readable Abstract

This paper addresses planning for integrated community energy systems under renewable generation uncertainty, multi-energy load variation, and equipment failures. It proposes a joint planning model of economy and reliability, formulated as a bi-level framework using the L-shaped method. Normal operating scenarios and N-k contingency scenarios are used to represent uncertainty and reliability requirements. A state similarity analysis method groups operational subproblems into similar-state sets so that only one representative scenario in each set needs full optimization, while solutions for other scenarios can be obtained by solving linear equations.

Key Findings

  • The L-shaped with state similarity method improves computational efficiency by more than tenfold.
  • Integrated flexible loads reduce energy storage requirements and improve system reliability.
  • The reported case study reduces investment cost by 16.53%, operational cost by 10.7%, and total cost by 14.71%.
