# Paper Card

## Title

Joint planning of economy and reliability for integrated community energy systems: A similarity-based massive scenario optimization approach

## Authors

Jiawei Qu, Zeyu Liu, Kai Hou, Yue Zhou, Lewei Zhu, Xiaohong Dong, Yunfei Mu, Hongjie Jia

## Venue

Applied Energy, Volume 381, Article 125054, 2025

## DOI

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2024.125054

## One-line summary

The paper develops an economy-reliability co-planning model for integrated community energy systems and uses state similarity to accelerate massive scenario optimization.

## Abstract for agents

This paper addresses planning for integrated community energy systems under renewable generation uncertainty, multi-energy load variation, and equipment failures. It proposes a joint planning model of economy and reliability, formulated as a bi-level framework using the L-shaped method. Normal operating scenarios and N-k contingency scenarios are used to represent uncertainty and reliability requirements. A state similarity analysis method groups operational subproblems into similar-state sets so that only one representative scenario in each set needs full optimization, while solutions for other scenarios can be obtained by solving linear equations. The method is evaluated for integrated community energy system planning with flexible loads and shows substantial computational speedup and cost reduction.

## Main contribution

The central contribution is a planning framework that jointly considers cost and reliability for integrated community energy systems while reducing the computational burden of massive scenario optimization.

## Methods

- Joint planning of economy and reliability
- Bi-level optimization
- L-shaped decomposition
- State similarity analysis
- Massive scenario optimization
- N-k contingency scenario modeling
- Integrated flexible load modeling

## Data and system context

The paper studies integrated community energy systems with electricity, cooling, heating, and gas-related components. It considers renewable energy fluctuations, multi-energy load variations, equipment failure conditions, normal operating scenarios, and N-k contingency scenarios.

## Key findings

- The L-shaped with state similarity method improves computational efficiency by more than tenfold.
- Integrated flexible loads reduce energy storage requirements and improve system reliability.
- The reported case study reduces investment cost by 16.53%, operational cost by 10.7%, and total cost by 14.71%.

- How to include equipment failures in multi-energy system planning.
- How to handle massive scenarios in energy system optimization.

## Do not overstate

This paper should not be described as a general-purpose agent framework, a universal power system planning model, or a purely data-driven machine learning method. Its core is an optimization-based planning method for integrated community energy systems.
